## Objective

Close the Phase-23 CR-01 deferral (D-01): turn `dnallm/__init__.py`, `dnallm/models/__init__.py`, and `dnallm/utils/__init__.py` into PEP 562 lazy facades so `import dnallm.preflight` stops pulling torch/transformers/datasets — and move the load-bearing utils-before-models shim ordering out of the root init and into the consumers themselves so a direct `import dnallm.finetune` or `import dnallm.models.model` keeps today's guarantee.

Purpose: the wizard must start textual immediately; today `import dnallm.preflight` costs 3.91 s and drags the training stack in before the first screen paints. This plan also removes the last runtime part of Phase 23's import-purity contract.

Output: three lazy facades with identical `__all__` resolution, shim-first imports in every module that resolves transformers modeling symbols, and a subprocess-based purity/parity/ordering contract file.

## Locked in-plan decisions

- **Keep eager in `dnallm/__init__.py`:** `__version__` (`dnallm/version.py` is a one-line constant) and `load_config` (`dnallm/configuration` imports only os/yaml/pydantic). Everything else resolves through `_LAZY`.
- **Heavy-name classification in `dnallm.models`:** `load_model_and_tokenizer` and `DNALLMforSequenceClassification` (`.model`), `DNAOneHotTokenizer` (`.tokenizer`), `FocalLoss` (`.losses`) are heavy; `MODEL_INFO`/`PRETRAIN_MODEL_MAPS` (`.modeling_auto`, stdlib-only) and all registry symbols (`.registry`, pydantic-only) are torch-free.
- **Heavy-name classification in `dnallm.utils`:** `_cuda_compat`, `_transformers_compat`, the `support` re-exports (`is_fp8_capable`, `is_flash_attention_capable`), and `training_plots` (matplotlib) go behind `__getattr__`; `logger`, `sequence`, `genomic_coords`, `genomic_intervals` stay eager.
- **The ordering guarantee is per-consumer, not root-owned.** D-01 trap (a) names `dnallm/models/__init__.py`; this plan additionally puts the shim import at the top of `dnallm/models/model.py` and of each heavy package init, because after laziness a direct submodule import no longer passes through the root init. Without that, the test-cuda CI legs lose the patch that `dnallm/__init__.py:24-30` installs today.
